## 📖 About the Project

**Gwent Checklist & Guide** is an independent fan-made project created to help players of *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt* organize their Gwent card collection and track their progress throughout the game.

The project provides an interactive checklist designed to make collecting cards easier, whether you are starting your first playthrough or working toward completing your entire collection.

Explore the available cards, organize your collection, filter cards by faction, and use the website as a companion while exploring the world of The Witcher.

The project is designed to be accessible directly through your browser, without requiring additional software.

## ✨ Features

- 🃏 **Interactive Card Checklist** — Keep track of the cards in your collection.
- 📚 **Collection Tracking** — Mark cards as collected and organize your progress.
- 🛡️ **Faction Filters** — Browse cards according to the available factions.
- 🔎 **Card Search** — Find cards more easily using the available search tools.
- 🗂️ **Card Categories** — Explore cards organized into groups.
- 🌍 **Bilingual Interface** — Use the website in Portuguese or English.
- 🧭 **Collection Guides** — Consult guides and information to help with card collection.
- 🗺️ **Maps and Locations** — Use location references to assist your exploration.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Access the checklist on desktop computers and mobile devices.
- 💾 **Browser-Based Access** — Open the website directly in your browser.

## 🃏 The Gwent Card Collection

Gwent is the collectible card game featured in *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt*.

Building a complete collection can require exploration, merchant visits, optional quests, and careful attention to cards that may become unavailable as the story progresses.

This project aims to make that process easier by bringing card tracking and collection resources together in one place.

Use the checklist to organize your progress and consult the available guides when you need help finding specific cards.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open the website using the button at the top of this page.
2. Choose your preferred language.
3. Browse the available cards and factions.
4. Use the search and filter options to locate specific cards.
5. Mark the cards you have collected.
6. Consult the available guides and maps when you need help with collection locations.
7. Continue updating your checklist as you progress through the game.

**Tip:** Keep the checklist open while playing to make it easier to track your collection.

## 🇧🇷 Sobre o Projeto

O **Gwent Checklist & Guide** é um projeto independente, feito por fã, para ajudar jogadores de *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt* a organizar e acompanhar sua coleção de cartas de Gwent.

O site oferece um checklist interativo, filtros por facção, pesquisa de cartas e recursos de apoio à coleta.

A proposta é facilitar a busca pelas cartas disponíveis no jogo, ajudando tanto quem está começando sua jornada quanto quem deseja completar a coleção.

O projeto conta com interface em português e inglês e pode ser acessado diretamente pelo navegador, tanto no computador quanto no celular.

### 🎯 Objetivos

- Facilitar o acompanhamento da coleção de cartas.
- Ajudar os jogadores a organizar as cartas obtidas.
- Tornar a consulta às cartas mais prática.
- Reunir ferramentas e materiais de apoio à coleta.
- Oferecer uma experiência acessível em português e inglês.
